Initial Screening Checklist

Review proposal completeness, required attachments, and format compliance.

Proposal follows the required CHED/URDS template, section order, and formatting guidelines.

All required forms, attachments, supporting documents, workplan, and budget documents are included.

Proposal is aligned with the university research agenda, priority areas, or college research direction.

Be specific when returning a proposal so the researcher knows what to correct.
