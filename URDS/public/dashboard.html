UEP Funded Research Process

Official workflow from proposal submission to completion.

View tracking
1

Submission of Research Proposal

Faculty Researcher submits proposal to the College Research Coordinator.

2

Screening and Evaluation

CRC, Dean, TWG, or assigned Evaluator reviews the proposal and gives recommendations.

3

Revision and Endorsement

Returned proposals are revised, then endorsed when compliant.

4

Agency In-House Review and UREC Review

URDS, evaluators, and UREC review the detailed proposal and budget.

5

Special Order and Notice to Proceed

Approved proposals receive official authorization to begin implementation.

6

Implementation, Monitoring, and Completion

Research is implemented, monitored, evaluated, and closed through terminal report submission.

Role Access Guide

Proposal Status Color Guide

This guide helps users understand the meaning of each proposal status color.

Gray - Draft / Not Started
Yellow - Submitted / Pending
Blue - Under Review / Evaluation
Orange - Returned for Revision
Green - Approved / Completed
Teal - Ongoing / Implementation
Red - Rejected / Terminated
