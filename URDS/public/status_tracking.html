Official Research Process Overview

This is the general UEP-funded research workflow used as the basis for proposal tracking.

1

Submission to College Research Coordinator

Faculty Researcher submits the proposal for initial checking.

2

Initial Screening and Dean Endorsement

The CRC screens the proposal before forwarding it for endorsement.

3

TWG / Assigned Evaluator Review

Technical reviewers or assigned evaluators review the proposal, encode ratings, and provide recommendations.

4

Agency In-House, UREC, and Director Review

Detailed proposal and budget undergo review before final approval.

5

Approval, Special Order, and Notice to Proceed

Approved research receives official authority to proceed.

6

Implementation, Monitoring, Evaluation, and Completion

The research is conducted, monitored, evaluated through appropriate forms, and closed through terminal report submission.

Proposal Status Color Guide

Use this guide to understand each color used in the tracking timeline.

Gray - Draft / Not Started
Yellow - Submitted / Pending
Blue - Under Review / Evaluation
Orange - Returned for Revision
Green - Approved / Completed / Issued
Teal - Ongoing / Implementation
Red - Rejected / Terminated
