When UREC Review Is Required

UREC review is usually needed when the study involves human participants, surveys, interviews, focus groups, personal information, vulnerable respondents, health-related data, animal subjects, or any activity with ethical risk.

UREC Routing Guide

For Director Review

Use this when the proposal satisfies ethics requirements or ethics clearance is acceptable.

Return for Revision

Use this when the proposal needs corrections, additional consent details, privacy safeguards, or clarification.

Rejected

Use this only when the ethical concerns are serious or cannot be resolved by revision.

Status Color Guide

Blue - Under Review / Evaluation
Orange - Returned for Revision
Green - Cleared / Forwarded
Red - Rejected / Terminated
